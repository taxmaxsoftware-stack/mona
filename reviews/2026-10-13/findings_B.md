## HIGH
1. **Module 6 — tiebreaker rules are wrong.** The error appears in the description, slide 6 (6aa6a76968a670f9442dcd9a), slide 7 (…cd9b), and the **Q4 answer key** (6a9724476ebc0245b90284b1).
   - Current text: "The parent, then higher AGI, then closeness of relationship."
   - "Closeness of relationship" is not a tiebreaker rule.
   - The correct rules (Pub 501):
     - A parent beats a non-parent.
     - Between two parents, the child goes to the parent the child lived with longer. AGI decides only when the time was equal.
     - If no parent can claim the child, the highest AGI wins.
     - A non-parent can claim the child only if their AGI is higher than the AGI of every parent who could claim the child.
2. **Module 6, slide 7 scenario** (child lived 9 months with the client) has no answer and points students to AGI.
   - Correct answer: the custodial parent claims the child.
   - The ex can claim the child (and the CTC/ACTC/ODC) only if the client signs **Form 8332**.
   - HOH, the EITC, and the dependent care credit **never** transfer to the noncustodial parent.
