## HIGH
1. **Module 15:** "If a client is audited … use Form 2848 to represent the client" is unqualified.
   - A preparer with only a PTIN has NO representation rights.
   - An AFSP holder may represent only on returns they prepared and signed, and only before revenue agents, customer service, or the Taxpayer Advocate Service — never before Appeals or Collection.
   - Q4 has the same problem.
   - Add Form 8821 for getting information without representation.
   - Sources: https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/understanding-tax-return-preparer-credentials-and-qualifications and https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i2848
2. **Module 14, Q4 (6aae9186fa9e0e4fcb60cb27) and the description:** "Form 2848 for authorized representation" has the same problem. Replace it with a PTIN-only scenario (answer: Form 8821, no representation).
