## HIGH
1. **Module 3 — wrong section for returning client records** (description, slide 2, objective 1).
   - The lesson says "§10.20/10.21 — return client records promptly". Returning client records is **§10.28**.
   - §10.20 covers responding to IRS requests for information.
   - §10.21 covers advising a client of an error or omission.
2. **Module 3 — outdated standard** (description, slide 4).
   - The lesson says "§10.34 — … realistic possibility standard". That standard was removed from Circular 230 in 2011.
   - Teach the current §10.34(a) standard: reasonable basis / unreasonable position under §6694(a)(2), meaning substantial authority, or a reasonable basis if the position is disclosed.
   - The removal is confirmed by The Tax Adviser (Jan. 2012). The irs.gov text was only partly readable.
3. **Module 4, Q3 (6aae9068b458eadc5b5b35bd) — misleading answer key.**
   - The keyed answer is "Per return for a due-diligence failure". The §6695(g) penalty is **per failure**: up to 4 per return, $650 each for returns filed in 2026, so up to $2,600 per return.
   - The lesson's own text says "each failure".

  - No penalty amounts are given: $635 (2025), $650 (2026), $665 (2027). Firm liability is missing.
